Field notes How Acme found its best hook. Three weeks, one board and every ad in its category. Acme searched for the creative its rivals ran longest. Long runs mean an ad pays for itself, so the board filled with the ones worth studying. What the board showed The ads that lasted opened on the product in a hand, not on a logo. They said the price in the first line. The first second sells the next ten. What Acme changed
  • Open on the product, in use.
  • Say the offer in the first line.
  • Cut every ad to under fifteen seconds.
